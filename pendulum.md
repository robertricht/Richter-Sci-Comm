The double pendulum is a classic example of **chaos** you can literally feel in your hands.  
Visitors set the initial conditions and release the system — and quickly see that even tiny differences in angle or timing
grow into dramatically different motion. This makes it a powerful, intuitive entry point into topics like sensitivity,
determinism, prediction, and measurement.

In facilitated settings, it also opens conversations about modeling: what we can measure, what we approximate,
and why “almost the same” can still lead to completely different outcomes.
