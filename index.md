I develop interactive science experiences that make complex ideas feel intuitive. As part of the non-profit collective [Experimental Stage Project](https://xstageproject.com/), I’ve co-developed and toured dozens of experiments and installations for science outreach—side by side with a strong crew of makers, scientists, and science-communication professionals. Working in this collective means I can tap into a large network for building, testing, and delivering robust experiences in real-world event settings.

Through my work as a lecturer for Digital Manufacturing at TU Berlin, I also have access to advanced workshops and expertise across wood, metal, textiles, electronics, and digital fabrication—supporting everything from rapid prototyping to durable, high-end builds for museums and science centers.

Beyond exhibits, I design live escape challenges with a science and technology core, and I develop and facilitate workshops, talks, and interactive shows on making, prototyping, and creative engineering—tailored to your audience, venue, and time format.
