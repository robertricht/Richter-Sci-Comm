**Escape Challenge Tourism** blends classic escape-room gameplay with innovative technologies such as **AI, augmented reality, and 3D printing**.  
In the story *“Bed & Breakfast with Urban Flair,”* participants arrive as guests in a stylish holiday apartment located in the basement of the Historic Archive. But before they can enjoy the minibar, they must solve interactive digital and creative puzzles—experiencing first-hand how technology can enhance the tourism and hospitality experience.

Along the way, guests follow a trail of clues and complete tasks that connect the physical space with digital tools.  
They encounter playful applications of AI, AR, and rapid prototyping, and discover how these technologies can enrich guest journeys—from storytelling and navigation to personalization and experience design—while keeping the focus on fun, immersion, and collaboration.
