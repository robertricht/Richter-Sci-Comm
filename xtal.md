			Create your own light sculpture. Shape it to your space.

			xtal is a modular lighting system developed by Robert Richter and
			Tobias Schubert. Flat LED-equipped circuit boards become
			three-dimensional light sculptures that customers assemble
			and configure themselves.

		<header class="major">
			<h2>Working prototypes</h2>
		</header>

		<p>
			We have built and refined substantially more than ten prototypes,
			exploring diamond, cubic and hexagonal geometries. A working
			prototype already provides room illumination. The same modular
			approach supports different spatial compositions, including
			hanging and freestanding configurations.
		</p>

		<p>
			The modules connect directly without separate connectors.
			Customers can determine the final form, reconfigure the structure,
			add modules or replace individual elements. Flat components
			allow compact packaging before assembly.
		</p>

		<header class="major">
			<h2>Development status</h2>
		</header>

		<p>
			The system is under development and is not yet a commercially
			released product. Current priorities include connection reliability,
			manufacturing tolerances, protected power input, safe operating
			limits and independent assembly tests.
		</p>

		<p>
			Our next step is to connect technical testing with customer
			validation: can first-time users assemble the kit independently,
			what are they willing to pay, and can we supply it with a
			positive contribution margin?
		</p>
