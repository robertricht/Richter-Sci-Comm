Nature Structure explores how rich, organic-looking forms can emerge from simple mechanisms and feedback.  
By tuning a system’s geometry, coupling, and “detuning,” small changes accumulate into spirals, interference-like structures,
and growth patterns that feel familiar from nature—succulents, shells, or Romanesco-like textures—yet remain fully explainable
as the result of straightforward rules.

The exhibit invites visitors to experiment: adjust parameters, observe how patterns change, and build intuition for
periodicity, resonance, and emergence—where complexity is not added, but **appears** through iteration.
