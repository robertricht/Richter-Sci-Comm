The **Quantum Escape Room** transforms abstract quantum concepts into physical puzzles and interactive story beats.  Quantum technologies are already starting to transform our world. To make their potential tangible and understandable for a broad audience, we run a live escape game that challenges players to solve exciting puzzles collaboratively. Participants step into a world where second-generation quantum technologies are in use today. Through play, visitors are encouraged to explore the remarkable properties of quantum mechanics.
